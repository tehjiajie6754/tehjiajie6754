## 💫 About Me

I'm Jia Jie — or just **JJ** — an AI undergraduate at Universiti Malaya. Day to day though, I think of myself simply as someone who loves building things that make sense.

I tend to gravitate toward spaces where logic meets human nuance:

- 🖥️ **At the desk** — I build software, often bridging engineering and UI design. I'm drawn to things that run fast, private, and local on devices, paired with interfaces that feel intuitive and calm. Beyond the flashy demos, I care about the unglamorous parts: clean structures, clear docs, and systems that don't break when you look away.
- 🎼 **On the podium** — I spend hours with orchestra scores, rehearsing with sections and learning how dozens of different voices come together into a single phrase. Conducting has taught me more about patience, listening, and balance than any textbook ever could.
- 🏸 **Outside of both** — You'll probably find me on a badminton court, or hunting down a quiet spot for iced dessert with friends.

> *I don't have everything figured out, but I enjoy the process of getting things right.*

Currently **open to internships**.
